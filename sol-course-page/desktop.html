Questions

Before you begin.

How long do I have access?
Forever. Your access never expires, so you can come back to it as often as you like.
Is this therapy or counseling?
No. Spark of Life is not a substitute for professional care. It is teaching and practical steps from people who have walked this road, and it works alongside a counselor if you have one.
Do I have to do it in order, or on a schedule?
Go in order, but on your own timeline. Some people finish in two weeks. Others take a lesson a week. There is no deadline.
What if it is not right for me?
You have 30 days to ask for a full refund. Just reach out, and we will take care of it personally.
