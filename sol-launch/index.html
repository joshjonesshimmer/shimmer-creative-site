Who Is Responding
  • Women 55 and over are the largest group visiting the course page from the ad.
  • Short videos (Reels) on Facebook and Instagram bring in nearly all of the visits.
  • The budget was raised to $250 on Wednesday to reach more people before the launch price ends.

What We Are Learning

  • The Spark of Life email list is the heart of the launch. Most enrollments came from people who already know David and Debbie.
  • Saying "online grief course" plainly, with a video preview, made the emails easier to act on. Monday's email had the best open rate of the week.
  • The ad is introducing Spark of Life to new people for about 30 cents a visit, and people who were new to Spark of Life are already enrolling.
